| 1 | should-fix | §4 TC-56-05, 10, 11; §5 M-56-02 (round-1 #4) | The "other write is lost too" proof is vacuous. The other write goes into `CREATE TEMP TABLE` created inside the same transaction. The pool also hands out a different connection after `run()` releases the runner, and temp tables are per session. | 3 | should-fix | §2 behavior #4, §4 TC-56-07b/07c (round-1 #2) | The runtime guard is still too vague to implement and test, and it does not enforce DoD #4. | 5 | should-fix | §2 #3, §3, §4 TC-56-06 (round-1 #3) | Three gaps remain. (a) The doc never says `recordDenied()` must not be called inside a `run` callback.
