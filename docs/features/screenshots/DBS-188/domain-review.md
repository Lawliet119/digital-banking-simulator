**What I checked and found correct:**
- **Emitter pairing:** `onCommitted` counts one decision per TRANSFER that won the claim, and one flag only when the level is not LOW. Deposits are never scored.
- **`[Role]` totals and dimensions:** `[Role]` is a total over all label combinations (`roleTotal`). Dimension labels come from fixed sets, so there is no PII.
- **Role and workers:** `APP_ROLE=worker` on ECS. Restarts and two worker tasks are handled by baseline deltas and `Sum`.
- **Thresholds and arithmetic:** the thresholds match docs/03 §14 and NFR-FRD-02. 45000/2250 is exactly 20.0, so the boundary works with `>`. The staged numbers are internally consistent.
- **Tests and namespace:** exact-string assertions on the expression catch the mutations 1000→1 and `>=`→`>`, and the threshold check catches 20→200. The namespace `DBS/Check-dbs188` passes the variable validation.
- **Plan feasibility:** the per-transfer limit (50M) and per-day limit (200M) allow ATO_DRAIN at 20M per sender. The rate-limit guard is off in `deposit-app` unless explicitly enabled.
- **P5:** writing into FRAUD_DETECTION_GUIDE §7 counts as P5, because §6.6 item 5 points to it.
