No money, ledger, ErrorCode or event-contract impact (infrastructure-only plus config composition). The env var names `REDIS_URL`, `REDIS_COMMAND_TIMEOUT_MS`, the `rediss://` rule for staging and production, and the pattern of mirroring `composeDatabaseUrl` all match `backend/src/config/validation.schema.ts`, `env.bootstrap.ts` and `compose-database-url.ts`. Several choices are consistent with ADR-10, ADR-11 and ADR-12, platform.md and docs/03 §12/§13:
- SG-to-SG rules
- private subnets
- no NAT
- CMK at rest
- the secret kept in `base` with recovery 0
- write-only token
